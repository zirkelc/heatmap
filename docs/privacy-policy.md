# Privacy Policy

Last updated: <DATE>

This policy is about the iOS app "<APP NAME>" (the "app") by Chris Cook ("I").

## Summary

- The app has no account and no sign-up.
- The locations that the app records stay on your device. I do not receive them, and the app does not send them to a server.
- The map in the app is from Google Maps. Google receives some technical data when the app loads the map (see below).

## Location data

The app uses the location of your device:

- while a recording runs, to save your location as points of a series, also when the app is in the background,
- to show your position on the map.

The app stores the recorded points in a database on your device. You can delete a series, or all series, in the app at any time. When you delete the app, iOS deletes all its data.

The app asks for the "While Using the App" location permission. You can change or remove the permission in the iOS Settings at any time.

## Google Maps

The app shows maps with the Google Maps SDK for iOS. To load the map, the SDK sends requests to Google. According to the privacy manifest of the SDK, Google collects crash data, performance data, product interaction data, a device identifier and a user identifier, for analytics and for the function of the map. Google does not use this data for tracking. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Other data

The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking. The app does not collect your name, email address or contacts.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
