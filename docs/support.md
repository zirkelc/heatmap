# Support

## Questions and problems

Open an issue at https://github.com/zirkelc/heatmap/issues. Please tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

## Frequent questions

**The app records no points in the background.** Check that the app has the location permission (Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Heatmap: "While Using the App" or "Always"). If you close the app from the app switcher, iOS stops the recording.

**The points are far apart.** In the app settings, set a shorter time interval and a shorter distance interval. The shortest values are 5 seconds and 5 meters.

**Where is my data?** Only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, iOS deletes the recorded data too.
