On a weekend city trip or a vacation in another country, you walk and drive to many places. You see a lot. But what did you not see? Which streets did you never walk? Which parts of the city haven't you seen yet?

Heatmap records where you go and shows it on the map. Your hotspots, the places where you spent the most time, show in red. Your blind spots, the streets and areas where you never went, stay empty. So you know where to go next to see something new.

Start a recording when you go out. The app saves your location while the recording runs, also when the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Each trip is a series. See one trip, or all of them together.

RECORD
• Start and stop a recording with one tap.
• Each recording is a series with a name, for example the city and the date.
• Continue an older series to add more points to it.
• Set how often the app saves a point: by time and by distance.

EXPLORE
• See all your series on one map, or select one series.
• Open a series to see its map, its number of points and its time range.
• Rename, merge and delete series.

PRIVATE
• No account. No sign-up.
• Your recorded locations stay on your iPhone. The app does not send them to a server.
• The app uses your location only while a recording runs and to show your position on the map.

The map is from Google Maps.
